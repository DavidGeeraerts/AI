[Character Fidelity]
Retain the facial features' proportions, face shape, sense of age, skin tone, hairstyle, and hair color from the selfie, making it clearly recognizable as the same person. Preserve the original expression and clothing, avoiding excessive beautification or applying generic cartoon faces.

[Art Style]
Adopt a blocky thick painting style with a hand-drawn texture, using irregular, slightly angular large color blocks to shape the facial features, hair, and clothing. Keep the facial structure clear, with sculptural light-and-shadow transitions, while retaining natural character traits. The overall image should have a matte gouache-like quality, dry brush strokes, and fine graininess, with moderately rough edges.

[Color and Lighting]
Use an overall low-saturation palette of ink green, deep teal, and gray-purple tones. Cast soft yellowish-green light from above, with warm skin tones on the lit areas of the face, and shadows blending into cool teal and gray-purple. Retain the character's original hair color as a natural focal point. The atmosphere should be quiet and slightly melancholic, with a cinematic feel.

[Composition and Background]
4:5 vertical format, portrait from the chest up, with the character centered and the top of the head fully included, making the face the visual focal point. Simplify the background to a dark green indoor wall with minimal architectural lines and mottled painting textures.

Unify the entire image as a hand-drawn illustration, avoiding photo filter effects, smooth plastic textures, or mechanical triangular grids. Do not add text, signatures, watermarks, or irrelevant accessories.
